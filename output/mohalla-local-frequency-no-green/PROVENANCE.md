# Mohalla Local Frequency — Provenance

- Original campaign platform: “Your neighborhood has a frequency.”
- Product role: Mohalla turns local signals into useful alerts, events, help, and market activity.
- Art direction: nocturnal editorial poster using midnight indigo, coral, saffron, electric blue, lavender, and warm peach.
- Explicit palette departure: no green and no white were used.
- Composition: deterministic HTML/CSS at 1080×1920 px, imported into Canva as one editable page.
- Typography: Syne ExtraBold for display copy and Poppins for supporting copy.
- Graphics: original inline vector geometry only; no third-party imagery, stock assets, or generated logos.
- Export preview: `mohalla-local-frequency.png` rendered at final platform dimensions.
- Canva design: `DAHPloILj40`.
